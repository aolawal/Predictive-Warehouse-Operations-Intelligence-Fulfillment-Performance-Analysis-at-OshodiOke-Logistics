# Predictive-Warehouse-Operations-Intelligence-Fulfillment-Performance-Analysis-at-OshodiOke-Logistics
This project transforms warehouse operations at Oshodi Oke Logistics from reactive reporting to predictive decision-making. Using logistics, inventory, labor, shipment, and returns data, it helps identify operational bottlenecks, predict backlog risks, and detect orders at risk of missing dispatch deadlines before service failures occur.

# Predictive Warehouse Operations Intelligence & Fulfillment Performance Analysis
### Oshodi-Oke Logistics | Data Analytics Project

**Driving operational efficiency, fulfillment reliability, and data-driven warehouse decision-making.**

## 1. Project Overview

Oshodi-Oke Logistics operates five warehouse facilities supported by 150 employees, processing 25,000 orders, 12,000 shipments, and over one million units.

Despite this operational scale, performance indicators reveal opportunities to improve delivery reliability, fulfillment quality, workforce productivity, and inventory efficiency.

This project analyzes warehouse operations to identify performance gaps, investigate operational inefficiencies, and uncover factors affecting fulfillment performance. It also proposes a predictive monitoring framework to identify emerging fulfillment risks and support timely management intervention.

## 2. Business Problem

Operational data indicates a 90% On-Time Delivery (OTD) rate, 1,000 late shipments, and a 12% return rate. These indicators highlight gaps in service reliability and fulfillment performance.

However, identifying these outcomes is not enough. Management needs visibility into the operational factors contributing to delays, returns, productivity variations, and inventory inefficiencies.

The central business challenge is the limited ability to connect warehouse performance indicators, identify emerging operational risks, and intervene before inefficiencies escalate into service failures and avoidable costs.

**Business Question:** How can Oshodi-Oke Logistics leverage operational data to identify warehouse inefficiencies, anticipate fulfillment risks, and improve service reliability, workforce productivity, and inventory utilization?

## 3. Project Objectives

The analysis focuses on five operational priorities:

1. **Delivery Reliability:** Identify shipment delay patterns, assess factors associated with late deliveries, and develop an early-warning approach for at-risk orders.
2. **Fulfillment Quality:** Analyze return patterns and identify opportunities to improve picking accuracy, packing quality, and product handling.
3. **Warehouse Performance:** Benchmark warehouse facilities using standardized KPIs to identify operational gaps and improvement opportunities.
4. **Inventory Efficiency:** Evaluate inventory levels and movement patterns to identify potential excess stock, slow-moving inventory, and utilization gaps.
5. **Workforce Productivity:** Assess employee productivity and throughput to identify performance variations and opportunities for better resource utilization.

## 4. Analytical Scope

The project applies descriptive, diagnostic, and proposed predictive analytics to five dimensions of warehouse performance:

| Operational Dimension | Key Performance Indicators |
|---|---|
| Service Reliability | On-Time Delivery, Late Shipments, Order Cycle Time |
| Fulfillment Productivity | Order Throughput, Picking and Packing Performance |
| Inventory Efficiency | Inventory Levels, Stock Utilization, Inventory Movement |
| Workforce Performance | Employee Productivity, Units Processed |
| Returns Management | Return Rate, Return Patterns, Return Reasons |

## 5. Proposed Predictive Framework

The project proposes a **Predictive Warehouse Performance & Service Risk Framework** to extend operational reporting beyond historical performance monitoring.

The framework is designed to support:

- **Shipment Risk Monitoring:** Identify orders showing potential signs of missing dispatch deadlines.
- **Backlog Monitoring:** Detect accumulating fulfillment backlogs and emerging warehouse bottlenecks.
- **Returns Risk Analysis:** Identify operational patterns associated with increased product returns.
- **Workforce Capacity Monitoring:** Highlight productivity variations and potential resource constraints.
- **Inventory Risk Monitoring:** Identify potential stock imbalances and inventory utilization issues.

The intended outcome is a transition from reactive performance reporting to proactive, risk-informed warehouse management.

## 6. Expected Business Value

The project aims to equip management with actionable insights to:

- Improve on-time delivery performance.
- Reduce avoidable fulfillment-related returns.
- Increase warehouse throughput and operational efficiency.
- Improve workforce and inventory utilization.
- Identify operational bottlenecks earlier.
- Strengthen data-driven planning and decision-making.

**Strategic Outcome:** A data-driven warehouse performance management approach that connects operational KPIs, performance insights, and predictive risk monitoring to support more reliable and efficient fulfillment operations.

## Predictive Insights & Operational Recommendations

### Orders-at-Risk Framework

The proposed framework aims to identify orders at risk of missing dispatch deadlines by monitoring key operational indicators:

- **Inventory Availability:** Stock shortages and backorders.
- **Warehouse Backlogs:** Orders awaiting picking, packing, or dispatch.
- **Labor Productivity:** Declining processing efficiency.
- **Dispatch Deadlines:** Orders approaching cut-off without sufficient fulfillment progress.

Combining these indicators enables operations teams to prioritize at-risk orders and intervene before service commitments are missed.

### Operational Risk Assessment

Current performance indicators highlight three critical areas requiring management attention:

| Risk Area | Key Indicators | Business Implication |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Service Reliability | 90% OTD; 1,000 late shipments | Delivery performance gaps |
| Fulfillment Quality | 12% return rate; 3,000 returns | Potential quality issues and reverse-logistics costs |
| Operational Capacity | 1M+ units; 150 employees | Need to assess workforce productivity and capacity utilization |

**Early-Warning Indicators:** Orders awaiting picking and packing, backordered quantities, warehouse utilization, labor productivity, and inventory availability.

Monitoring these indicators provides earlier visibility into potential bottlenecks before they affect service performance.

### Management Recommendations

| Timeline | Priority Actions |
|:---|:---|
| **0–90 Days** | Deploy dispatch risk monitoring, improve picking and packing accuracy, and introduce standardized warehouse KPI scorecards. |
| **3–12 Months** | Develop workforce and capacity forecasting, establish productivity benchmarks, and implement backlog monitoring. |

### Strategic Recommendation

Management should prioritize developing a **Predictive Warehouse Operations Control Tower** to integrate:

- Order Risk Monitoring
- Backlog Forecasting
- Workforce and Capacity Planning
- Warehouse Productivity Intelligence
- Service-Level Risk Prediction

**Expected Business Impact**

- Fewer late shipments.
- Reduced avoidable returns.
- Lower operating and overtime costs.
- Improved workforce productivity.
- Better inventory and capacity utilization.
- Stronger customer satisfaction and profitability.

### Executive Conclusion

Oshodi-Oke Logistics has significant operational scale, but its 90% On-Time Delivery rate, 1,000 late shipments, and 12% return rate highlight opportunities to improve fulfillment reliability and operational efficiency.

The priority is to move beyond retrospective reporting toward proactive operational management.

By connecting warehouse KPIs with early-warning indicators and predictive risk monitoring, management can identify emerging bottlenecks, prioritize at-risk orders, and intervene before service failures occur.

**The competitive advantage is not simply processing more orders. It is identifying operational risks earlier, making faster decisions, and consistently delivering on customer commitments.**
